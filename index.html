About Me

A young developer & digital creator

"I build digital experiences, communities, and systems that bring ideas to life."

I'm Apdulaziz Mohamed Karar, a 20-year-old multidisciplinary developer and digital creator, currently in my final semester at Somali National University. My work spans web development, graphic design, Discord server development, Discord bot development, and — most recently — cybersecurity.

I love turning ideas into real digital experiences. That means building websites and digital platforms, shaping online communities, automating Discord systems with custom bots, and creating visual designs that give each project its own identity.

Most of my practical experience comes from building real projects for gaming and community environments — especially Somali gaming communities, eFootball communities, SA-MP communities, and streaming communities. Working with real communities has taught me to solve real problems, learn new technologies quickly, and keep improving what I build.

How I Work

Every project I take on follows the same simple, reliable path — understanding the purpose first, then designing, building, testing, and improving.

01

Understand

I start by understanding the purpose, the audience, and what the project really needs to achieve.

02

Design

I design a solution that fits — the right structure, look, and experience for the goal.

03

Build

I build it carefully, keeping the code and design clean and organized as I go.

04

Test

I test the result, check how it behaves in the real world, and fix what isn't right.

05

Improve

I keep refining and improving the experience long after the first version is live.
